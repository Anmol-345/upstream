Frequently asked questions

What is LIVE?

Blockchain live streaming: media for the people, by the people. Today: live sport unlocked by holding $LIVE instead of paying a subscription. Next: Stream to Earn, where anyone can go live and earn from the platform's creator fees.

Is this IPTV?

No. IPTV resells somebody else's feed through somebody else's app. Every stream here runs through our own J4 infrastructure and connects directly to the chain. No third parties, no subscriber database, no middleman.

What is Blockstreaming?

Blockchain streaming: the right to watch is a token in your wallet instead of an account on a server, and the right to earn is a channel in your name. LIVE is the first service built on it.

What does “for the people, by the people” mean?

Streaming quietly became renting. Your subscription is permission, not property: the price goes up, matches move to another app, your region gets blocked, and the day you stop paying it is all gone. We are building the other model. The pass to watch is a token you hold in your own wallet, like a disc on your shelf: nobody can revoke it, repossess it or paywall it twice. And the channels themselves belong to the people who stream, not to a platform that rents them an audience.

How much does it cost?

There is no monthly fee. Hold about $50 of $LIVE; while you hold it, you watch. The token trades on the open market, so its price is set there.

Where can I watch?

Anywhere with a browser: desktop, Android and iOS. Native apps for the App Store and Google Play are coming soon.

What if a channel is blank?

Some feeds go dark between events. Pick another from the rail; the player also fails over between two delivery networks on its own.

Is there an account?

No. A wallet signature is the whole login. Nothing is stored about you beyond a short-lived signed session that proves you cleared the gate.

How do I get a channel?

Sign in with your wallet and pick a username: your channel is live at usestream.tv/u/your-name, searchable and followable from day one. Anyone can make one. Going live on it is invite only for now; it opens to everyone with the apps.

What is Stream to Earn?

Anyone can become a streamer. Go live, entertain, build an audience. A live leaderboard ranks streamers on views, viewer interaction, engagement and overall performance, and your rank sets your share of the creator fees. You don't need to be famous, you need to keep people watching.

What about bots?

A serious anti-bot system is part of the launch. Anyone caught inflating viewers or engagement is permanently banned. The leaderboard only works if the numbers are real.

Can I add a channel?

Yes. Request it on the wishlist and upvote what others have asked for. The lineup is built from those requests.
